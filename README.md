# *Supply Chain Analytics Project*

## *Overview*
Analyzed supply chain and retail data to evaluate sales performance, inventory management and operational efficiency using *Excel, SQL, Tableau and Power BI*.

## *Objectives*
* Analyze sales trends across different time periods  
* Monitor inventory levels and stock status  
* Identify top-performing products and stores  
* Understand regional and customer-level performance  

## *Key KPIs*
* Sales (MTD, QTD, YTD)  
* Sales Growth & Daily Trends  
* Product-wise & Store-wise Performance  
* Region & State-wise Sales  
* Inventory Levels & Stock Status  

## *Tools Used*
Excel | SQL | Tableau | Power BI  

## *Project Files*
📂 Project Files & Dashboards: https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1epwnP9AhaypvDI10mBgUGiavVsl5qtl6?usp=sharing

## *Insights*
* Sales vary across regions and product categories  
* Inventory availability directly impacts sales  
* Top stores contribute major revenue  
* Customer purchase patterns differ by location  

## *Conclusion*
Provides insights to improve *inventory management, sales performance, and overall supply chain efficiency*.
